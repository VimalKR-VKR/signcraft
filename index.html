How to add your signature

Pick your email client below and follow the steps. Takes less than 2 minutes.

1

Click "Copy for Gmail / Outlook"

Hit the purple button above. This copies your signature as rich formatted content — not raw code — so Gmail understands it perfectly when you paste.

2

Open Gmail Settings

Go to mail.google.com. Click the ⚙ gear icon in the top-right, then click "See all settings."

3

Create a new signature

Stay on the General tab. Scroll to the "Signature" section. Click "Create new" and give it a name like "My Signature".

4

Paste directly into the text box

Click inside the signature text area and press Ctrl+V (Windows) or Cmd+V (Mac). Your formatted signature — with colors, fonts, and layout — will appear exactly as designed. No HTML editing needed.

If it looks plain after pasting, try right-click → "Paste as rich text" or use a Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave).
5

Save changes

Scroll to the bottom of Settings and click "Save Changes." Your signature will now appear automatically in every new email you compose.

1

Copy your signature HTML above

Click "Copy signature HTML" to copy your signature code to the clipboard.

2

Open Signature settings

Desktop: File → Options → Mail → Signatures. Web: ⚙ Settings → View all Outlook settings → Mail → Compose and reply.

3

Create a new signature

Click "New", give it a name, then in the editor right-click and select "Edit Source" to switch to HTML view.

4

Paste and save

Paste the HTML into the source editor. Click OK, then OK again to save.

For best results in Outlook, the plain text version is often more reliable.
5

Set as default

Under "Choose default signature," select your new signature for both New messages and Replies/forwards.

1

Copy plain text version

Apple Mail works best with plain text. Click "Copy plain text version" above.

2

Open Mail preferences

Open the Mail app. Go to Mail → Settings → Signatures tab (or Preferences on older macOS).

3

Add a new signature

Select your email account on the left, then click the + button to create a new signature. Give it a name.

4

Paste and close

Click into the preview area on the right and paste with Cmd+V. Close the window — Apple Mail saves automatically.

5

Assign to your account

Drag your signature to your account on the left, then set it as default from the "Choose Signature" dropdown at the bottom.
